### Activity

Initial project discussion, identification of a real-world problem, and selection of the project idea.

### Discussion

The team discussed different problems faced by students within the Thapar community. One major problem identified was the difficulty students face when buying and selling used items such as books, electronics, furniture, and other useful products.

Currently, students may depend on WhatsApp groups, personal contacts, social media, or other informal methods to find buyers and sellers. These methods can make it difficult to discover relevant listings, organize information, and establish trust between users.

### Decision Made

After discussing the problem, the team decided to develop **ThaparTrade**, a peer-to-peer marketplace designed specifically for the Thapar community.

The proposed system will allow students to list items for sale and allow other members of the community to discover and connect with sellers in a more organized environment.

### Initial Scope

* Platform for the Thapar community.
* Students can create and view listings.
* Listings contain relevant information about the item.
* More organized alternative to scattered informal selling channels.
* Users can report unauthorized or misleading listings.
* Administrators can review reported listings and take appropriate action.
