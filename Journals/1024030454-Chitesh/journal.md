# Week 1: 3 August – 9 August 2026

During the first week, I participated in the initial planning and discussion of our Software Engineering project. Our team discussed the idea of developing **Thapar Trade**, a campus marketplace intended for students and members of the Thapar community.

I contributed to understanding the problem faced by students while buying and selling used items within the campus. We discussed the need for a centralized platform where students could list products they wanted to sell and browse products listed by other members of the campus community.

We discussed the initial requirements of the system, including user registration and login, product listings, marketplace browsing, product details, and basic administrative functionality. I also participated in discussions regarding the target users and the major interactions that users would have with the system.

We started organizing the project requirements that would later be used for the Software Engineering proposal, documentation, and system design.

I gained a clear understanding of the project idea, problem statement, target users, and initial functional requirements of Thapar Trade.

# Week 2: 10 August – 16 August 2026

During the second week, I worked with my team on requirement analysis and system planning for Thapar Trade. We examined the requirements identified during the first week in greater detail and determined the major modules required in the system.

I contributed to discussions related to the **authentication and marketplace modules**. We planned the authentication module to support registration, login, logout, and user profiles. The marketplace module was planned to allow users to browse, search, filter, and view available product listings.

I also contributed to discussions regarding listing management functionality. Users would be able to create listings, edit their listings, delete listings, and mark products as sold after completing a transaction.

We also discussed administrative functionality for maintaining the reliability of the marketplace. A reporting mechanism was planned through which users could report inappropriate or problematic listings for administrator review.

### Outcome

I helped define and organize the major functional modules and requirements of the Thapar Trade system for further design and implementation.

During the third week, I contributed to the system design and Software Engineering documentation of the Thapar Trade project.
